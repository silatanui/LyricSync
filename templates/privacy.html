Privacy notice

Privacy is the default: your project is not listed publicly unless you choose Public.

What the studio processes

Uploaded audio, video, lyrics, timing data, style settings, and generated exports are processed to provide the editor and rendering workflow. During testing, project data is stored by this application so you can return to your work.

Public projects

Only projects explicitly marked Public are selected for the homepage community shelf. Private projects are excluded from that list. Publishing is optional and should be used only for work you are comfortable sharing.

Browser preferences

The studio may keep simple interface preferences (such as light or dark theme) in your browser so the editor feels the same next time you visit.

AI providers

When enabled, transcription and lyric organization may send relevant audio or lyric text to configured AI providers. Do not upload sensitive material unless you understand and accept that processing path.

Your choices

You can keep projects private, avoid uploading sensitive content, and contact Tanui with privacy questions or partnership requests.
