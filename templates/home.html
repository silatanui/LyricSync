The idea

A calmer bridge between raw audio and a finished visual.

Most lyric video workflows scatter the work across transcription tools, timing spreadsheets, design software, and export settings. LyricSync brings those decisions into one studio where the song, words, timing, and visual treatment stay together.

Being explored

More language support

Better multilingual transcription, language-aware lyric formatting, and alignment for songs beyond English are active areas of research.

Being refined

Smarter musical phrasing

Lyric breaks should follow the song's phrasing, not arbitrary character limits. The alignment and stanza model is still being improved.

Being tested

More expressive visuals

Expect richer motion, more backgrounds, stronger previews, and tools for treating each lyric passage as its own visual moment.

A portfolio project with a larger purpose

Built by Tanui to explore useful AI automation.

LyricSync Studio is part of my portfolio: a practical experiment in making AI feel less like a black box and more like a capable collaborator. I am interested in projects where automation removes repetitive work while people remain responsible for judgment, taste, and direction.

I am also ready to contribute to a research team or a group that genuinely believes the world should benefit from AI. That means building carefully, acknowledging what is unfinished, and learning from the people who use the work.

Good automation should give people more room to think, make, and decide.

Tanui
